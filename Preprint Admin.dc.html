One page, plus one per piece

{{ pageDirtyMsg }}
the recommendation

Have one page. A bio, then everything you have written, listed in full — no nav, no landing page that sends the reader elsewhere to start reading. Each article still gets its own URL, because writing that cannot be linked to cannot be cited, and a working paper nobody can cite is a PDF nobody found.

So: a single index that is the site map, and one page per article. Research and writing become sections of that page, not destinations. Add a second page only when the thing is genuinely not writing — a CV, say.
