Plates & markers

Three plates, pressed with the page — full strength, structural, never translucent. Three markers, added afterwards by hand — translucent, always a field behind type. Nothing in the system may be both.

A marker is a painted box and stacks in source order: with display leading, lift the ink above onto its own layer rather than loosening the leading.

04

Icons & pointers

A 16-unit grid, 1.35px stroke, square caps, three angles, and one optional filled square where a glyph needs a dot rather than a stub of stroke. Colour enters only where a glyph reports a state — confirmed, blocked, informational — and never to decorate. Pointers work the other way round: the shape never changes at all. point is the plain arrow with the same silhouette cast in plate 3 behind it, so a clickable surface reads as raised, and and the cast shortens the instant the button goes down.

hot {{ c.hot }}

Hotspots are contract: a surface may repaint a pointer, never move its point. The lift is the only state change with no shape change: point casts 1.1px, pressed casts 0.5px. The difference is deliberately almost nothing — just enough that the hand registers the surface giving way, not enough to look like the shadow was taken away. The swap is instant rather than animated — a reader clicks hundreds of times, and a transition here is felt as lag. Hold the mouse down over the icon grid above. All of them are dropped on touch.

The primary surface. Select a passage and right-click (or long-press on touch) to highlight it in one of the three markers, or copy a link that opens on that exact sentence. Click an existing mark to recolour or remove it. Hover a sidenote or its anchor and the pair lights up faintly.

Research · market design

On the reluctance of systems to break their own rules

Why consistency is cheap, exceptions are expensive, and a system that has never violated itself has probably never been used for anything difficult.

rev 15 · 2026-07-26 2 figures · 1 interactive

Every system I have written starts strict and ends apologetic. The strictness is easy: pick four radii, three faces, six slots, and refuse. What is hard is the third month, when a screen arrives that the system describes accurately and serves badly — and the honest move is to break something.

The reluctance is not aesthetic, it is administrative. A violation has to be explained to someone later, and explanation is expensive, so teams quietly prefer a page that is uniformly mediocre to one that is excellent with a footnote.1

The proposal is administrative too: write the exceptions into the contract, give each a budget, and mark them so nobody has to guess whether a deviation was intent or drift.2

A budget, formally

Let v be the number of licensed violations visible in one view. Attention does not accumulate; it divides.

w(v) = 1v · wmax(1)

At v = 1 the exception carries full weight. At v = 2 each carries half — worse than none, because the reader now has two candidates for the important thing and no way to choose.

The three plates are assigned by position, not meaning, so syntax colour carries no semantic claim:

small screens · notes

Below 820px the margin column has nowhere to go, so it stops being a margin. Each sidenote folds into the flow directly after the paragraph that cites it, keeping its number and its blue rule — a note, not a footnote. Nothing is collapsed behind a tap: a note you have to open is a note nobody reads.

small screens · marking

There is no right-click, so the menu comes from the selection itself: lift your finger and it appears under the passage. Same three markers, same share action, 44px targets. Long-press and drag to adjust the selection first — the menu waits.

small screens · figures

Figures already span the full column, so they simply become full width. Tables keep their real column widths and scroll sideways rather than reflowing into unreadable stacks. Interactive controls sit below their figure so a thumb never covers the thing it is changing.

what does not change

The measure. Sixty-six characters is sixty-six characters — on a phone that means the type does not shrink and the margins do not vanish; the column simply becomes the screen. Body stays 17px, the drop cap stays, the rules keep their weights.

Law 00 — two breaches cancel

Not discouraged. Cancelled. Attention divides rather than accumulates, per equation (1).

A breach used twice is an undeclared token

The second appearance stops being a breach. Name it, give it a slot, write its states — or stop.

The law must survive the breach

If breaking it makes it look arbitrary, the law was wrong. Delete it instead of licensing around it.
