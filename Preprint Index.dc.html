I work on what allocation rules do to the people they bind — matching, priority, and the exceptions a mechanism quietly needs to survive contact with the world. Doctoral researcher in Vienna, at the seam between market design and the software that decides who gets what.

This is not a blog. Some of what follows is work I would defend at a seminar, some is a thing I built or taught myself over a weekend, and some is a position I have decided to hold in public. The coloured rule beside each title says which.
